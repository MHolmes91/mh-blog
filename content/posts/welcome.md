This is the first post on the production blog. It exists to validate the theme, search index, taxonomy pages, archives, related posts, and DigitalOcean static-site deployment.

## Why this setup

The blog content lives in its own repository, while the theme remains a separate Hugo Module dependency. That keeps content publishing separate from theme development.
